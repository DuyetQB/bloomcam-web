## Why plant timelapses feel magical

Plant growth is slow — but timelapse reveals a living story. A cinematic plant timelapse turns patience into shareable art.

## What you need

- **BloomCam** — a smart plant timelapse camera designed for desks and shelves (not surveillance)
- Stable light (window light or a soft lamp)
- A clear background that matches your aesthetic

## Setup checklist

1. Place BloomCam at eye level with your plant.
2. Connect the BloomCam mobile app over WiFi.
3. Name your plant and start capture.
4. Let BloomCam adapt capture intervals as growth changes.

## Creator workflow

BloomCam exports vertical video ready for Reels, TikTok, and Stories — no timeline editing required for basic shares.

## Best subjects

Monstera unfurls, pothos trails, succulents, and fiddle leaf new leaves all look incredible in timelapse.

## Summary

BloomCam is built for **plant lovers and creators** who want cinematic growth stories without a studio setup.
